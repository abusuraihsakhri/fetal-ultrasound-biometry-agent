Interpretation and limitations

This is a numerical educational tool, not a validated clinical decision-support system. Formula-derived weight has measurement uncertainty and cannot establish fetal growth restriction, a percentile, gestational age or an anomaly. Assessment requires appropriate ultrasound technique, dating, growth reference, Doppler and clinical context. Do not use this output alone for medical decisions.

All calculation and CSV export occur locally. No measurements are transmitted or saved by this page. Avoid entering patient-identifying information.

Reference: Hadlock FP et al., Am J Obstet Gynecol 1985;151:333–337. See also ISUOG mid-trimester ultrasound guidance.
